# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

- Chess coaching students of FM Luis Chan (Melbourne and Glen Waverley), using the site between lessons.
- Adult club players improving on their own.
- General online visitors who arrive for the puzzles and games.

Parents were not confirmed as an audience.

## Product Purpose

Chess Parfait is FM Luis Chan's chess training site. It gives players structured, coach-made practice between lessons: weekly puzzles, endgame drills against human-like and perfect opponents, endgame strategy lessons, daily training plans and chess variants. Success is a player who keeps coming back, trains regularly and can see themselves improving.

## Positioning

Training designed and curated by a FIDE Master coach, not a generic puzzle feed: hand-picked weekly puzzles, tablebase-verified endgame lessons and daily plans modelled on how the coach trains.

## Operating Context

- Players earn points from weekly puzzles, endgame drills and finished daily training plans; points set a level with a rank title.
- Weekly puzzles come in three difficulties named Piece of Cake, Hard Tart and Cherry Bomb.
- Practice opponents include Maia bots with dessert names (Strawberry Cake, Lemon Tart, Mr. Cherry) and Stockfish.
- Accounts are free; signed-in players get a public leaderboard rank, a forum identity and a profile.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- React and Vite single-page app on Cloudflare Pages, with Supabase for accounts and data.
- Player data available to the profile: username, avatar, join date, linked email, points, level and rank title, solved puzzles (week, difficulty, title, points, date), completed endgames, pawn game record, training plan, training streak and calendar.

## Brand Commitments

- Name: Chess Parfait. Coach: FM Luis Chan.
- Dessert vocabulary for difficulty levels and practice bots.
- All copy in Australian English.

## Evidence on Hand

Real player data only (points, puzzles, records) from each account. There are no testimonials, ratings or third-party statistics to show on the profile; none may be invented.

## Product Principles

1. Show players their progress and the single next step, not everything at once.
2. Serious chess training with warmth: never childish or gamey, never a cold dashboard.
3. Everything on screen should feel specific to Chess Parfait and chess, not a template.
4. Keep screens calm enough for younger students to find their way.
